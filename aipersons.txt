
Constrictor (Default, Legacy AI)

Goal - Suffocate. Max pressure on enemy silvers, rewards closing circles, avoids self-freezes.

Signals - Strong enemy silver penalty, elevated circle pressure, tiny tolerance for minor self-loss if it freezes them.

Rupture

Goal - Fracture the opponent into many disconnected Swan groups, then constrict.

Signals - Bonus for increasing enemy group count, moderate circle pressure, mild willingness to trade tempo for future cuts.

Blizzard

Goal - Force freezes quickly, even via opportunistic trades.

Signals - Very high freeze reward, lighter self-freeze penalty than Constrictor, small sacrifice bias to accept short-term strife for a freeze.

Librarian

Goal - Clean positional play. Keep your own silvers high, reduce theirs methodically.

Signals - Big weight on your own silvers, big penalty on theirs; least interested in circle theatrics.

Swarm

Goal - Rapid development to many active swans, then press.

Signals - Early-game nudge toward Swan placement; moderate circle pressure; slightly down-weights early Stones.

Fortress

Goal - Build sturdy, choking structures and win on containment.

Signals - Highest circle-pressure emphasis; conservative about self-freezes; slower, heavier positions.
