Linith AI Personalities

Difficulty controls how broadly and deeply the AI considers moves. Personality
controls which strategically close move it prefers. Every personality obeys
the same rules, forced wins, and unique tactical defenses.

Doctrinal (Default)

Goal - Play balanced, canonical Linith without a separate strategic bias.

Signals - Uses the objective evaluator as written. Doctrinal is the zero-bias
baseline against which all other personalities are measured.

Constrictor

Goal - Suffocate opposing Swans while keeping its own formations safe.

Signals - Strong enemy-liberty pressure, closing pressure, containment, and
self-preservation. It accepts only a tiny concession when that advances a
concrete freeze.

Rupture

Goal - Fracture the opponent into disconnected Swan groups, then constrict.

Signals - The strongest bonus for increasing enemy group count, moderate
closing pressure, and limited tolerance for positional concessions that create
a real cut.

Blizzard

Goal - Force freezes quickly, including through controlled opportunistic trades.

Signals - The strongest freeze urgency, lighter additional self-preservation,
and the greatest tolerance for a small concession that produces a concrete
freeze. It still cannot ignore a forced loss.

Librarian

Goal - Play cleanly and methodically, preserving its own silvers while reducing
the opponent's.

Signals - The strongest liberty balance, stable mobility, low appetite for
circle theatrics, and careful self-preservation.

Swarm

Goal - Develop rapidly to many active Swans, then press with the larger formation.

Signals - The strongest early Swan-development preference, reduced interest in
quiet early Stones, moderate pressure, and active mobility.

Fortress

Goal - Build sturdy, choking structures and win through durable containment.

Signals - The strongest structure and containment preferences, high
self-preservation, territorial control, and deliberately slower development.
