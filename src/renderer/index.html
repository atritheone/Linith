Start Sun places first Swan, Moon places second (not adjacent), then Moon has the first turn.

Activity Select an empty tile; either place a Swan (white) (maximum six on the board per player; once both players have six Swans on the board, each turn has two actions.) or place a Stone (black) • Select one or more Swans to move them one tile (green is a legal move; red is not; cyan/purple to deselect) or push them, if they are enemy Swans and are adjacent. Adjacent Stones follow; shared Stones stay; Stones adjacent to any enemy Swan stay.

Victory Choking an enemy Swan's surrounding empty tiles (silvers) encircles it. Encircled Swans freeze, becoming immovable and unable to spawn. Freezing an opponent's Swan grants one extra action per Swan frozen. When the last active Swan is encircled, the player loses the game. If both players’ final active Swans are encircled during the same turn, the game ends in a draw.
